## 6. The two hardest Q&A questions, with the best honest answer today

**Q1. "Your images are cropped, augmented lab sets with over a million near-duplicate pairs, and none of them is a farmer's phone photo on a sheet of paper in Kenya. What accuracy will Noor get, and how do you know 'not sure' fires when it should?"**

Best honest answer: "We do not know Noor's accuracy, and we say so (`RESPONSIBLE_AI.md` section 10). We found 1,244,500 near-duplicate pairs by perceptual hashing and split by cluster, so the same leaf cannot sit in both train and test. We then test on a Ugandan set from another country (424 images, also augmented, so likely optimistic). We report the drop from in-domain to Uganda rather than one pooled number: [numbers from EVALUATION.md]. The abstention threshold is set on a validation split and we publish the coverage it costs: at that threshold we answer X% of leaves and are right on Y% of those. The 10-leaf design follows the Nuru field study in Kenya and Tanzania, where single-leaf accuracy was 21 to 59% and six leaves gave 74 to 88%, on cassava, so it is design precedent and not our result. Every referral goes to an officer who confirms or corrects it, and the dashboard already exports those corrections as local labelled data for retraining."

Until EVALUATION.md is filled, the team can only answer the first and last parts. That is the main reason Evidence scores 2.

**Q2. "After Noor presses send, what actually happens? There is no SMS gateway, no cooperative has agreed to receive these messages, no officer has signed off your thresholds, and the officer visits twice a year."**

Best honest answer: "Today, nothing happens automatically. In the demo the officer pastes the SMS into the dashboard, and the screen says 'simulated gateway'. No cooperative or officer has agreed to anything yet. Every cut-off in rules.json is marked 'assumption, officer to confirm', because we found no Kenyan incidence threshold. The design point is triage. With one agent per 1,380 farmers (MoALD 2025), the officer's few visits should go to the plots that sent a referral or where the tool abstained, and the referral carries the cooperative member and plot number, which can later carry a KIAMIS ID. Before any real use we need a named officer to sign off the rules, a data-sharing agreement with the cooperative and a field test on the real two-phone pattern (`RESPONSIBLE_AI.md` section 9). We are showing a tool that is ready for a pilot, not a service that is already running."

This answer is credible only if the docs stop over-claiming elsewhere (fix 1).
