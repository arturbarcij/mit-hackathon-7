## Open points for review

1. The 2-of-10 rust trigger maps a 20% figure meant for systemic sprays to a small, biased sample. Officer to confirm or change.
2. Thresholds are counts, not shares. A check with fewer than ten photos uses the same counts.
3. Healthy leaves with one or two unsure leaves go to `ask_officer`, not `healthy_all`. Conservative on purpose.
4. Kikuyu lines are short and few. A native speaker should rewrite them and add the rest, including `consent_main`, which we left out rather than guess.
5. Swahili word choices to check: `kutu` (rust), `madoa ya kahawia` (brown eye spot), `ugonjwa wa buni` (coffee berry disease), `viwavi wachimba majani` (leaf miner), `programu` (the tool).
