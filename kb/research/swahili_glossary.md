# Swahili glossary: coffee disease and spraying terms

Owner: research agent. Hand-off to content-voice (C2 translations) and docs (LANGUAGES.md). Accessed 3 Oct 2026. Raw pages are in `raw/swahili/` (header with URL and date).

## Read this first

- **Almost all the Swahili coffee text I could fetch is from Tanzania, not Kenya.** Tanzanian sources: `mitiki.blogspot.com` (its own text says "mazingira halisi ya ki Tanzania"), the Sokoine University of Agriculture (SUA, `mkulima.sua.ac.tz`) booklet "Kilimo Bora cha Kahawa", `mkulimajembe.blogspot.com`, `kilimohaitz.blogspot.com` and `maishadaily.wordpress.com`. Plantix is a global app with Swahili text. I tagged every entry with its origin: **TZ** (Tanzania), **GL** (global, origin unknown), **KE** (Kenya).
- **Kenyan Swahili sources found: few, and none from KALRO or Infonet.** Pass 3 added a Kenyan marketplace listing (Umoja) that uses **kutu ya majani ya kahawa** and **ugonjwa wa matunda ya kahawa (CBD)**, and Radio Jambo (Kenyan radio) uses **magonjwa ya kahawa**. Taifa Leo coverage of coffee is about prices and payments. Infonet Biovision (Kenya) has no Swahili coffee page. CABI Plantwise Swahili factsheets could not be fetched. KALRO KEEP is a JavaScript app and returned no text. Kenyan TV Swahili hits were videos with no usable transcript.
- The four Tanzanian blog pages (maishadaily, mkulimajembe, kilimohaitz, mitiki) and the SUA booklet are **copies of one text**, so they count as one source, not five.
- So every term below is **a Swahili term attested in East African sources, not confirmed as Kenyan usage.** The Swahili reviewer (STATUS L3) must confirm Kenyan usage before release. Where the Kenyan word is likely different, I say so.
- **Do not copy spray schedules, product names or counts from these pages.** The Tanzanian pages give schedules ("three to four times a season", "within three weeks before the rains") and brand names that do not match the Kenyan review (S03). I quote them only to show how the terms are used.
- Quotes are exact, including source spelling slips.
