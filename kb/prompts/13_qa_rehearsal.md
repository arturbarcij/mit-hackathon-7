# Prompt 13: Q&A rehearsal (Claude or Cursor, judge agent chat)

Use at Sun 11:00 and again after pass 3 reports. Paste into a fresh chat with `kb/agents/judge.md` loaded.

Task: act as a sceptical panel of three (a machine learning researcher, an agronomy or extension specialist, a development economist from the World Bank). Using only the repo and the latest reports in `kb/judge/`, `kb/redteam/`, `kb/math/`, `kb/agronomy/`, `kb/security/`, write `kb/pitch/QA_REHEARSAL.md` with 20 hard questions, five per panelist plus five on responsible AI and privacy.

For each question give:
1. The question as a judge would ask it, one sentence.
2. The best honest answer the repo supports today, max 60 words, spoken-style.
3. The evidence path (file and line, or report name) for every number in the answer.
4. A flag if the honest answer is weak, and what would fix it before 13:30.

Must include at least these: why 97% in domain but the Uganda result is poor; what happens when the photo is not a coffee leaf; why not an SMS or hotline; who confirms the advice and who pays for the officer's time; what the tool cannot see; how Kikuyu is handled and what is machine-generated; what the cooperative has to do for this to work; what happens to the data on a lost phone; why the advice is a rule table and not a model; what would you build with another month.

Rules: no invented numbers; if a figure has no source, write "assumption" or "unproven". Plain British English, no em dashes, no marketing language. Reply in five lines when done: done, not done, blocked on, unsure about, next.
